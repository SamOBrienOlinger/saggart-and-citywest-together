# Fluent-language review checklist

Automated captures are evidence for review, not approval. A fluent reader must review each language before it can be marked complete.

## Review scope

Review the Home, Citywest support guide, Facts, Quiz introduction and one complete quiz attempt. Compare each page with the English original.

For every language, record:

- reviewer name or agreed identifier, review date and language proficiency;
- whether IPAS, LAIT, DDLETB, CSO, HSE, PPSN and GDPR remain unchanged;
- whether service names, eligibility cautions, emergency wording and source labels keep their meaning;
- whether buttons, navigation, quiz feedback and privacy choices are natural and unambiguous;
- any correction required in the English source, protected-term handling or explanatory note.
