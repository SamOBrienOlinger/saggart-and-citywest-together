# Community-information user test

This short review checks whether newcomers and established residents can find accurate local information. It supplements browser automation; it does not replace accessibility testing or fluent-language review.

## Participants

Include newcomers and established residents. Where possible, include people who use a phone as their main device and people who use translated information. Do not ask participants to disclose immigration status, health information or other sensitive details.

## Tasks

Ask participants to work from the homepage without step-by-step coaching:

1. Find where a Citywest resident can ask for help locating local services. Identify the responsible organisation and its original source link.
2. Find English-language or adult-education support and explain what the linked provider offers.
3. Find one Saggart population fact, its date and the CSO source. Ask what geographic area they think the figure describes.
4. Start the quiz after rejecting optional storage. Explain whether the score will be saved.
5. Choose a language, review the privacy choice, and explain what will be sent to Google before continuing.
6. Find how to contact the group about community activities and how to view local history photographs.

## Record

For each task, record completion, first route chosen, hesitation or wrong turn, wording that caused confusion, and whether the participant trusted the source. Record the device, viewport and assistive technology when volunteered. Do not record names or sensitive personal information in the public repository.

## Review decisions

Prioritise changes when participants cannot distinguish the community guide from the responsible service provider, misunderstand the CSO geography or date, cannot dismiss the privacy choice, or cannot reach support and participation information on a narrow screen. Record proposed copy or navigation changes as separate, reviewable issues rather than treating a single session as proof of a general outcome.
