Privacy

How this website uses your data

Your quiz score

The quiz saves your best score and a small setting on this device. It does not create an account or send your score to us. Clear this site’s browser data to remove it.

Translation

The Languages control does not load third-party translation code when you open a page. Google Translate is contacted only when you choose a language, and the translated copy opens in a new tab. Google may then process the public page content and set cookies under its own privacy terms. This site does not store your language choice.

Contact by email

The contact page opens your own email app. This website does not collect or store your message. If you send an email, your message and email address are shared with Saggart & Citywest Together through the email services involved. Only include information needed for your question.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not track your visits or use advertising cookies of its own. The optional Google Translate service may use cookies after you choose a language.

Links to other websites

Links to sources open other websites in a new tab. Those websites have their own privacy rules.
