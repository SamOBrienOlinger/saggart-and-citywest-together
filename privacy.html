Privacy & cookies · Updated 11 November 2026

How this website uses information

You can read the site and play the quiz without creating an account, allowing Google Translate or saving a score. Optional features are off until you choose them. The buttons at the bottom of every page let you change your choices or clear this site's saved score.

Who to contact

Saggart & Citywest Together is the community group responsible for enquiries sent to it. For privacy questions, information requests or concerns about content or photographs, email saggartcitywesttogether@gmail.com. Please identify the page or enquiry concerned, without sending unnecessary identity documents or sensitive personal information.

Browsing and hosting

The public website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Loading pages involves sending connection information, including your IP address and browser request, to the hosting provider so it can deliver and secure the service. This is different from optional advertising or audience-measurement tracking. See the GitHub privacy statement for its processing, retention and international-transfer arrangements. We cannot delete records held independently by a provider through a browser-settings button.

Your browser-storage choices

These controls use local storage, not a tracking cookie. Consent to optional device storage is separate from any lawful basis needed for personal information. Rejecting optional features does not prevent ordinary browsing or playing the quiz.

saggart-and-citywest-together:privacy:v1 — necessary preference record
Remembers the notice version, time of choice and your two permissions. It is written when you make a choice, contains no randomly assigned visitor identifier and is not sent to us. Choices are treated as valid for 180 days, then requested again on a subsequent visit or check. Browser data does not physically disappear while a browser is closed.
sctQuizHighScore and sctQuizSchema — optional quiz saving
Store your best score and the format version on this device only when “Remember my quiz score” is allowed. They are not uploaded to us. The controls remove them when you withdraw permission, clear your score, or the site next detects expired or invalid permission. Without permission, the best score exists only in the current page's memory and is lost on reload.

Earlier versions used kykQuizHighScore and kykQuizSchema, also used by Know Yellowknife on the same GitHub Pages origin. Saggart no longer reads or writes those legacy keys and does not import an ambiguous old score. Clearing all browser data for this origin can also remove preferences belonging to other projects hosted here.

Optional Google Translate

No Google translation widget is loaded automatically by these controls. With JavaScript enabled, selecting a translation link without permission opens the privacy settings first. After allowing translation and selecting a language, you leave this site for a translated copy in a new tab. Google receives the public page address/content and connection information such as your IP address, and may use its own cookies. Its separate choices and retention rules apply: Google privacy notice.

“Cookie settings” lets you withdraw permission for future translation-link activations here. It cannot close an already opened Google tab, undo information already sent, or delete cookies on Google's domain. External links can also be opened directly using browser commands; leaving this site is governed by the destination's privacy controls. Machine translation may contain mistakes.

Email and community enquiries

The contact link opens your email application; it is not an embedded web form. An email you choose to send contains your address, message and any attachments, and is handled through the email services involved. The purpose is to respond to the enquiry and organise the community activity you asked about, not to enrol you automatically in marketing.

Handling ordinary enquiries is based on the group's legitimate interest in communicating with people who contact it, subject to their rights and reasonable expectations. Where another basis is required for a particular activity, that should be explained when the information is requested. Messages should be kept only as long as needed to resolve the enquiry, organise the relevant activity or meet an applicable legal requirement. Ask the group for the retention position for a particular enquiry. Do not send health, immigration, identity-document or other sensitive details unless necessary and an appropriate route has been agreed.

Photographs, external services and links

Public content and photographs can themselves contain personal information. Contact the group with a page link to raise a correction or removal concern. Facebook, Instagram and source links lead to independent websites; a plain link does not load their social-media widget. Their own policies apply when you visit them. Third-party resources, when present on a page, can receive connection data when your browser requests them.

This privacy update adds no analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. Adding a new embedded service requires a fresh review, an accurate notice and blocking any non-essential storage or tracking until consent is given; this notice is not permission to introduce it silently.

Your rights and concerns

Where personal information is processed, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Rights have conditions and exceptions. You can withdraw consent for consent-based processing without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Contact the group using the email above. Requests will normally require a response within one month, subject to the GDPR's permitted extensions and identity checks proportionate to the request.

You can complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission. The browser controls only manage this website's optional features; they do not replace requests concerning email, photographs or providers' records.
