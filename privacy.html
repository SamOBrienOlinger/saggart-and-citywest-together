Privacy

A minimal-data prototype

Quiz storage

The quiz stores only your best score and a data-schema version in your browser’s local storage. It does not create an account or send that score to a server. Clear this site’s browser data to remove it.

Contact demonstration

The private-review contact form validates entries in your browser but does not transmit or store them. A production privacy notice must be added when a real form service is configured.

Analytics and cookies

This MVP does not include analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies.

External links

Source links open third-party websites governed by their own privacy practices.
