About the project

Learning that strengthens local belonging

Saggart & Citywest Together is a community organisation based in Saggart, County Dublin. This site extends its work as a welcoming local learning resource.

Our mission

The project fosters connection, belonging and cultural understanding for people across Saggart and Citywest. It makes local knowledge easier to discover through concise learning, trusted sources and a replayable quiz.

Editorial approach

The content uses public information from South Dublin County Council, the Placenames Database of Ireland and official heritage resources. It has not undergone formal civic or heritage review and does not speak for those organizations. Every learning card and quiz explanation links to its source.

Project origins

This resource carries forward the learning-through-play concept developed by Allies in Action, preserving the accessible functionality of Know Yellowknife and Know Dublin with new local content.
