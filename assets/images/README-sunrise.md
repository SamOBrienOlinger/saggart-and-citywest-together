# Homepage sunrise artwork

- `hero-saggart-citywest-v12.jpg` remains the original homepage illustration and final still frame.
- `hero-sun-logo.jpeg` is the logo supplied by Sam on 4 November 2026, unchanged. The SVG animation clips its square corners to fit the sun.
- `hero-sunrise-sky.webp` is an auxiliary sky layer, generated by editing the original illustration and encoded as WebP. Only a soft circular mask around the original sun is displayed; the rest of the original landscape stays visible.

The sky edit requested removal of only the yellow sun disk centred near (1167, 149), preserving the surrounding sky, clouds, lighting, composition and landscape. This layer disappears when the animation completes. The sunrise lasts three seconds and the logo fades over the following 1.8 seconds. Reduced-motion visitors and browsers unable to load the animation assets receive the original still image.
